description: Distinctive, intentional visual design for new interfaces.
source: https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-design
---

Use the subject matter to drive visual choices. Establish a compact palette, deliberate typography, responsive layout, accessible focus states, reduced-motion behavior, and one memorable visual idea. Review the result against the brief before shipping and avoid generic dashboard defaults.
