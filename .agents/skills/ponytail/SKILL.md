---
name: ponytail
description: Minimal implementation discipline and YAGNI guidance.
source: https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail/tree/main/skills/ponytail
---

Understand the path first, then choose the shortest implementation that satisfies the request. Reuse existing patterns, prefer platform features, avoid speculative abstractions, keep the diff small, and leave one runnable check for non-trivial behavior. Do not simplify away accessibility or error handling.
